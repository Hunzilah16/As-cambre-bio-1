Question 2 easy [8 marks]
Part (a) [2 marks]

Fig 1.1 shows a potential divider circuit containing a fixed resistor and thermistor. 

Fig 1.1

 Explain what happens to Vout as the temperature of the thermistor increases.

Mark Scheme (2 marks):

As the temperature increases:

  • The resistance of the thermistor decreases; [1 mark]

  • So Vout increases; [1 mark]

[Total: 2 marks]

Potential dividers are all about the sharing of the potential from the battery (hence the name). When the resistance of the thermistor decreases, this means the voltage through it also decreases (from V = IR where I is the same for both resistors). 

However, the battery has a set voltage. Therefore, the voltage of the fixed resistor must increase so the combined voltage of the thermistor and fixed resistor remains the same. Vout is the reading of the voltage from the fixed resistor.
