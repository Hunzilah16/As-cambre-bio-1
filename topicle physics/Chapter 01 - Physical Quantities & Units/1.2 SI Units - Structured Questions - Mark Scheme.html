Mark Scheme (4 marks):

(i) 0.71 GJ in standard form and 3 s.f. is:

  • 1 GJ = 1 × 109 J

  • 0.71 GJ = 0.71 × (1 × 109 J)

  • 0.71 GJ = 7.10 × 108 J (3 s.f.) [1 mark]

  • 1 mm = 1 × 10−3 m
