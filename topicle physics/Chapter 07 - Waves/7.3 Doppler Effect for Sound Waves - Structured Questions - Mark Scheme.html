Part (d) [1 mark]

State the equation as it would be used to calculate the frequency of an observed sound moving away from an observer.

Mark Scheme (1 marks):

When an observed sound is moving away from an observer:

  •  fo = fs vv + vs{"language":"en","fontFamily":"Times New Roman","fontSize":"18","autoformat":true} [1 mark]

[Total: 1 mark]

If you forget which is + and which is − just think about it logically.

A sound moving away from the observer decreases in pitch and therefore frequency. For the frequency to decrease, the denominator needs to be larger, hence (v + vs).

A sound moving toward the observer increases in pitch and therefore frequency. For the frequency to increase, the denominator needs to be smaller, hence (v − vs).
