7.3 Doppler Effect for Sound Waves

Syllabus Section: 7. Waves Paper Type: Multiple Choice (Paper 1) Total: 14 Questions (14 Marks)
Question 1 easy [1 mark]

Which of the following statements are true?

A

When a source emitting a sound wave moves away from an observer, the observed frequency increases
B

When a source emitting a sound wave moves away from an observer, the observed wavelength increases
C

When a source emitting a sound wave moves towards an observer, the observed frequency decreases
D

When a source emitting a sound wave moves towards an observer, the observed wavelength increases
Mark Scheme & Explanation:

The correct answer is B because:

  • When a source emitting a sound wave moves away from an observer, the waves are stretched

    • Therefore to an observer the wavelength increases

Option A is incorrect because

  • When a source emitting a sound wave moves away from an observer, the observed frequency decreases as the waves are stretched

Option C and D is incorrect because

  • When a source emitting a sound wave towards an observer, the waves are compressed

  • Therefore the observed wavelength decreases and the frequency increases
